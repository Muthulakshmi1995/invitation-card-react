Important prototype behavior from the user's latest instruction:

- Navbar and Footer are shared and remain visible when the route/content changes.
- Home and Wedding Cards are separate content pages under the same shared Navbar/Footer.

Observed pages/screens:
- Home: hero, laser-cut section, unique/exclusive cards, theme-based invitation, offers, simple/affordable wedding cards, summer offer, invitation collections, happy customers, how it works, footer.
- Wedding Cards: WEDDING CARDS heading and 12 category cards in a 4-column desktop grid.
- Search: Everything You Need, to Plan your Dream Wedding; search/filter UI and recommendation cards.

- Wishlist: Wishlist heading and The blue Wedding Cards, Rs. 72.25.
- Cart: Product / Qty / Unit Price / Tax / Price, SKU KSN0054, totals, minimum 100 items warning, Continue Shopping, Checkout.

Source limitation:
The recording shows the dropdown labels and hover states, but it does not show the destination content screens after clicking each individual Special occasions / Theme / Scroll / Digital dropdown option. Those destination screens are therefore not claimed as exact prototype-derived screens here.
